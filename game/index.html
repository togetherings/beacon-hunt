BeaconHunt

Look at a street photo and find where in the world it was taken.

  • Look around the photo for clues: signs, cars, plants, roads, buildings, the weather.
  • Click the map, then press Guess. Each photo is worth up to 1,000 points.
  • Finding the country: the right one gets 1,000, a country next door 500, the same part of the world 300 and the same continent 100. Finding the place: the closer your pin, the more points.
